## What to Check Before Buying a Plot

Before purchasing a plot in Gaya Dobhi Road, buyers should conduct proper due diligence. Important points to verify include:

* Ownership and title documents
* Plot boundaries and dimensions
* Land-use classification
* Road access and approach
* Required approvals and permissions
* Electricity and water availability
* Drainage and surrounding infrastructure
* Applicable local development regulations
* Any existing legal claims or encumbrances

Getting the documents reviewed by a qualified professional can help buyers avoid unnecessary complications during the purchase process.
